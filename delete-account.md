# Delete Your BakeMaster Pro Account

**Last updated: 4 August 2026**

BakeMaster Pro users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

## How to request account deletion

Send an email to:

**zahers642@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**BakeMaster Pro Account Deletion Request**

Please send the request from the email address connected to your BakeMaster Pro account and include:

- Your full name
- Your registered email address
- Your bakery or company name
- A clear request to delete your BakeMaster Pro account and associated data

## What will be deleted

After the request is verified, we will delete or anonymize, where applicable:

- User account information
- Email address and display name
- Bakery or company membership information
- Recipes, inventory, production, employee, and business data associated with the account
- Cloud data associated with the account, where technically and legally possible

## Information that may be retained

Some information may be retained when required for:

- Legal or regulatory obligations
- Accounting and tax records
- Payment and subscription records
- Fraud prevention
- Security investigations
- Resolving disputes

## Processing time

Account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days after identity and account ownership are verified.

## Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your BakeMaster Pro account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

You must cancel the subscription separately through your Google Play account.
