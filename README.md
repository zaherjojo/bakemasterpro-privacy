# Privacy Policy for BakeMaster Pro

**Last updated: 4 August 2026**

BakeMaster Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a bakery management application developed and operated by Zaherjojo.

This Privacy Policy explains how BakeMaster Pro collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access or use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, BakeMaster Pro may collect or process the following information:

### Account information
- Email address
- User name or display name
- Authentication identifiers
- Account role, such as owner, manager, or employee

### Bakery and business information
- Recipes and ingredients
- Inventory records
- Products and production records
- Orders and customer information entered by the user
- Employee attendance and work records
- Reports, notes, settings, and other bakery-management information

### Purchase and subscription information
Subscriptions and payments may be processed through Google Play Billing.

BakeMaster Pro does not directly receive or store full payment-card details. Google Play may provide purchase status, subscription status, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and related billing information needed to activate and manage paid features.

### Device and technical information
The app and its service providers may process:
- Device type and operating-system version
- App version
- Device or installation identifiers
- Diagnostic information
- Crash reports and performance information
- Security and authentication logs

### Camera, photos, files, and microphone
When the user chooses to use related features, BakeMaster Pro may request access to:
- Camera
- Photos or images
- Files and documents
- Microphone or audio recordings

These permissions are used only to provide user-requested functions, such as attaching recipe images, scanning information, uploading documents, or using voice features.

### Artificial-intelligence features
When a user chooses to use an AI-assisted feature, prompts and any voluntarily attached text, image, file, or audio content may be transmitted to the relevant AI service provider for processing and generating a response.

Users should not submit confidential, highly sensitive, or legally restricted information through AI features.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users and control access
- Provide bakery-management features
- Save and synchronize app information
- Manage recipes, inventory, production, orders, employees, and reports
- Process and verify subscriptions
- Provide customer support
- Improve app stability, performance, and security
- Detect abuse, fraud, technical failures, or unauthorized access
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

## 3. Services and Data Sharing

BakeMaster Pro may use third-party service providers to operate its features.

These may include:

- Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, databases, hosting, notifications, diagnostics, and related backend services
- Google Play for app distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and payment processing
- AI service providers when the user voluntarily uses AI-assisted features
- Technical service providers needed for app security, hosting, communication, or error reporting

Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to provide these services, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or maintain app security.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 4. Firebase

BakeMaster Pro may use Google Firebase services. Depending on the Firebase services enabled, Google may process authentication information, installation identifiers, diagnostic information, app activity, and information stored by users through the app.

Firebase processes information according to Google's applicable terms, security measures, and privacy documentation.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Information may be stored locally on the user's device and, where cloud features are enabled, on secure third-party infrastructure.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the app and its features
- Maintain user accounts
- Fulfil subscriptions and transactions
- Resolve disputes
- Meet legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and security obligations

Information stored locally may remain on the device until the user deletes it, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

## 8. User Choices and Permissions

Users can:

- Decline optional device permissions
- Remove permissions through Android device settings
- Choose not to use optional camera, microphone, file, image, cloud, or AI features
- Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us

Some features may not work when the permissions required for those features are disabled.

## 9. Children's Privacy

BakeMaster Pro is intended for bakery owners, managers, employees, and other business users.

The app is not specifically directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 10. International Data Processing

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the user's country, including locations where our service providers operate.

Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data processing.
